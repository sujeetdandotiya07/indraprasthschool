Our Story & Foundation

Transforming Schooling into an Inspiring Journey of Discovery.

Indraprasth Public School was founded with a definitive vision: to bring world-class educational standards, modern pedagogical techniques, and digital literacy to students in Gohad and neighboring regions.

Located Behind the LIC Office at the bustling Gohad Chauraha, our institution bridges the gap between conventional moral grounding and modern technological expectations. From kindergarten exploration to advanced secondary board and competitive exam preparations (JEE, NEET, CUET), our structured educational path ensures students develop deep analytical thinking and strong ethical values.
