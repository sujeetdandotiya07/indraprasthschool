Structured Progression

Our 4-Tier Academic Framework

From early sensory play to rigorous secondary board targets, each wing is thoughtfully crafted to suit developmental stages and academic rigors.

Early Childhood Education • Ages 3 to 5

Pre-Primary Wing (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

In the foundational phase, we stimulate curiosity, develop fine motor coordination, and foster a lifelong love for learning through joyful experiential play. Children explore the world without the burden of rote memorization.

Phonics & Early Vocabulary Phonetic sounds and interactive language songs to instill speaking confidence.
Sensory & Motor Skills Puzzles, clay modeling, pattern recognition, and balancing games.
Audio-Visual Storytelling Engaging smart screens displaying animated folk tales and moral parables.
Safe Activity Play-zone Soft playing corners under full CCTV surveillance and caring female attendants.
Foundational Competencies • Classes 1st to 5th

Primary Wing (Classes 1 to 5)

Transitioning to structured conceptual understanding. We focus on mathematical logic, reading fluency in English and Hindi, practical environmental science, and computational thinking from Class 1.

Core Mathematics & Abacus Speed mathematics, mental arithmetic, and visual geometry principles.
Early Computer Literacy Keyboarding, basic typing, MS Paint, and kid-friendly logic tools.
Experiential EVS Hands-on plant care, weather observations, and civic cleanliness habits.
Handwriting & Elocution Dedicated calligraphy and public recitation periods in school assemblies.
Exploratory & Analytical Phase • Classes 6th to 8th

Middle School (Classes 6 to 8)

Middle school bridges primary intuition and rigorous academic disciplines. Students engage in separate Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories, alongside third language introduction (Sanskrit) and coding algorithms.

STEM Lab Demonstrations Real-world scientific apparatus handling under supervised faculty.
Python & Web Fundamentals Basic algorithmic coding, flowcharting, and HTML styling basics.
Olympiad Foundation Targeted preparation for Science Olympiad (NSO) and Math Olympiad (IMO).
Debate & Social Inquiries History, Geography projects, and active participation in youth parliaments.
Board & Competitive Focus • Classes 9th to 12th

Secondary & Senior Secondary (9 to 12)

Intensive board examination guidance combined with career aptitude counseling. We provide stream specialization across Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Arts with continuous mock board tests and doubt clearing.

Board Exam Target Batches Past 10 years question banks, answer writing techniques, and time management drills.
JEE / NEET / CUET Foundation Synchronized objective test series matching national competitive standards.
One-on-One Doubt Desks Daily evening doubt clearing counters with subject specialists.
Dedicated Career Mentoring Guiding admissions into premier colleges, NDA, defense, and commerce avenues.
Modern Pedagogical Approach

How We Teach at Indraprasth Public School

Education at our Gohad Chauraha campus moves away from rote cramming, placing student inquiry and real-world execution at the epicenter.

Smart Digital Boards

Every classroom is equipped with interactive audiovisual smart panels, turning 3D biological organs, geometric figures, and planetary models into vivid reality.

Practical Coding Labs

Empowered by technology leadership (SD SOFTECH initiative), students learn computational thinking, algorithms, and real problem-solving from early years.

Continuous Assessment

Weekly chapter reviews, diagnostic tests, and monthly progress tracking to catch weaknesses early and offer swift academic remedies.

Regular Parent Dialogues

Frequent Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) with actionable student portfolios, ensuring parents stay aligned with academic progress.

Senior Secondary Specialization
