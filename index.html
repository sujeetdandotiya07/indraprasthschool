About Us

Empowering Minds at
Indraprasth Public School

Indraprasth Public School, located conveniently Behind the LIC Office at Gohad Chauraha, is dedicated to nurturing holistic growth. We combine traditional values with modern technological advancements to equip every child with confidence, character, and intellect.

Computer & Tech Labs

Practical coding and computer literacy from early classes.

Safe Campus

Full CCTV coverage, disciplined and secure environment.

Curriculum & Programs

Academic Excellence

Balanced curriculum tailored for foundational growth and competitive examination success.

Pre-Primary (Nursery - UKG)

Activity-based playful learning, sensory motor skills development, phonics, and communicative confidence building.

  • Child-Friendly Playrooms
  • Audio-Visual Smart Modules

Primary & Middle (1st - 8th)

Core academic subjects (Mathematics, Science, English, Social Sciences) with a strong focus on logical reasoning.

  • Regular Practical Demonstrations
  • Quizzes & Olympiad Preparation

Secondary & Senior Secondary

Focused board examination curriculum along with foundational guidance for competitive exams.

  • Dedicated Career Counseling
  • Weekly Tests & Doubt Sessions
